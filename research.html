My current research focuses on governance and executive labor markets in financial institutions. Building on this work, I am interested in how regulation and technological change shape organizational adjustment, bank behavior, risk, and financial stability.

Working Papers

Board Gender Quotas and Career Spillovers in Banking

This paper examines how binding board gender quotas affect women’s representation and career dynamics across senior leadership in banking. Using BoardEx career histories matched to BankScope/BankFocus and staggered European quota reforms, I find that quotas substantially increase women’s board representation and generate career spillovers across the senior leadership hierarchy, with effects differing across levels and career margins.

Stability versus Disruption in the Market for Bank Executives

with Stefano Colonnello and Shuo Xia

This paper studies the labor market for bank executives in the United States and Europe over 2000–2021. Despite substantial transformation in banking, executive labor markets remain remarkably stable, while turnover, reallocation, and internal appointments reflect distinct patterns of selection across individuals and banks.

Banking Transformation Since 2000: A Literature Review and Stylized Facts from Listed Banks

This project combines a review of the literature with cross-country stylized facts on banking transformation since 2000. The evidence documents substantial changes in bank scale, operating structure, capitalization, and profitability, alongside persistence in the traditional deposit-funded intermediation model.
